## 🎯 Educational Philosophy: "Math You Can Touch"

All worksheets in this series follow three core principles:

1. **LEGO Native Units**: We use **holes and studs** (1 hole = 8 mm) rather than abstract millimeters or inches so 9-to-14-year-old students can grab parts directly from their SPIKE Prime or MINDSTORMS bins and build what they see on screen.
2. **Visual & Intuitive Physics**: Concepts like Grashof's theorem, transmission angle, gear ratios, and torque are explained using relatable metaphors (windshield wipers, playgrounds, umbrellas) rather than walls of algebra.
3. **Deep-Dive Ladders**: For high school students, FRC mentors, and inquisitive kids, each worksheet links to the formal mechanical engineering theory (Freudenstein equations, trigonometry, vector loops) so learning never hits a ceiling!
